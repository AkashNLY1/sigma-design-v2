Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about SIGMA.

What is SIGMA Coin?

SIGMA is a token on the TRON network that you can buy with USDT.

How do I buy SIGMA?

Go to Get Coins, connect your wallet, install TronLink if needed, search for “Sigma Token”, and pay with USDT.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wallet is your account. Registering on this site is optional and lets our team help you.

Which wallet should I use?

TronLink is the supported wallet.

Is crypto risky?

Yes. Prices can change quickly and you can lose money. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

Why did I get a call from your team?

If you registered and have not bought yet, our team may call to answer questions. You can ask us to stop at any time.
